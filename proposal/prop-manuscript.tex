\begin{abstract}
  This proposal presents a neural-network (NN) framework for enforcing hard constraints in the presence of uncertain constraint functions.
  HardNet++ provides a systematic approach for constructing NNs whose outputs satisfy prescribed hard constraints. 
  However, its direct application requires the functions defining the constraints to be known.
  To address constraints containing unknown (uncertain) functions, additional NNs are introduced to estimate the unknown components and incorporate them into the constraint-enforcement architecture. 
  The proposed approach will be investigated in both neuro-adaptive-control and deep-learning frameworks.
  Theoretical and numerical studies will be conducted to examine constraint satisfaction, approximation performance, and closed-loop behavior of the resulting framework.
\end{abstract}
